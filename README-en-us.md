 # Repository: ModRedReign

This repository houses the mod NeoForge “Red Reign” for Minecraft.

## Minecraft versions supported
* 26.1.2.

## Features:
* 3 New recipe types, including one that adds HP cost to crafts;
* Melted Beeswax Liquid, which can restore the player’s health;
* 2 Decorative blocks;
* A new type of Totem of Undying that restores health equal to the life steal from the wearer;
* 4 Bloodstained relics that punish the wearer to grant them more HP;
* 9 Life infused relics that fortify the player;
* 2 New swords with unique abilities;
* 3 New harmful mob effects;
* 1 New story-related structure;
* Play and experience the story that Red Reign wants to show you;
* Achievements and a Modonomicon book that guide the player;
* World events that, when triggered, affect all players:
  * Effect: “HP regeneration from any source is reduced by 60%”;
  * Effect: “The player’s passive HP regeneration is disabled”;
  * Effect: “All animal drop loot tables have a reduced chance of dropping meat”;
  * Effect: “All meat restores health beyond hunger, including from other mods”.
* 1 Final Boss;
* Additional: Customized tooltip borders.
